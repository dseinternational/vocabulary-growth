## 1. Verification of the review's findings

**Duplicate TD administrations (review §3.4) — reproduced exactly.** The 2026-06-15 Wordbank export carries 28 exact full-row copies (100,527 rows, 100,499 distinct). Reconstructing each registered frame from the raw table: 22 excess exact source copies entered VG11's 18,522-row frame, 3 entered VG12's 7,052 and 2 entered VG13's 6,358. The two VG13 pairs are the review's cited rows — Floccia child 61556 at 17 months (`wordbank_administration_data.csv` lines 34,684-5) and ByersHeinlein child 90038 at 12 months.

**Calibration language scope (review §3.6) — reproduced exactly.** `univariate_frame()` and `bivariate_frames()` in `scripts/kappa_conditional_calibration.py` called the loader without `definition.td_languages`, so the calibration frames were the English-only default: 16,235 rows against VG11's registered 18,522, 5,997 against VG12's 7,052, and 5,406 against VG13's 6,358 (understood). After the fix the calibration frames match the registered frames exactly (18,500 / 7,049 / 6,356 — the registered frames net of the duplicates above).

**Reporting-estimand errors (review §3.3) — confirmed.** The VG11/VG12 median-trend bands are built from `y_plot`, which draws a new child effect (`_delta_subject_marg`) and Beta-Binomial noise per draw — a predictive distribution for one new child — while both captions called them "uncertainty in the average study's average child, not the range children occupy", close to the opposite of what is plotted. The "average child in the average study" gloss for zero-effect curves is inexact under the logit link: a zero effect is the median-type child, and `E[inverse_logit(f + delta)] != inverse_logit(f)` wherever `tau > 0`. The headline `kappa` row, relabelled from "Spread between children" by #234, still read "Spread across same-age administrations" for fits that carry child effects, where `kappa` is residual variation conditional on child and study.

**Stale VG12 extrapolation warning (review §5.4) — confirmed.** `posterior_summary.csv` is written after `trim_reported_ages` applies `report_max_age_understood = 25` (#228), so the rendered table stops at the last observed comprehension month; the callout claiming its last rows are extrapolation described the pre-#228 table.

**VG13 BFMI and curvature prose (review §§6.2-6.3) — confirmed.** The report asserted that low E-BFMI "degrades tail exploration, not the centre" (a localisation the diagnostic cannot support) and read GP inertness as evidence that the trajectories are close to straight, although the shared length-scale prior spans roughly 6-18 months over a ten-month window and the anchored, basis-projected GP has little effective curvature capacity — [202608060900](202608060900-three-prior-conflicts.md) §5 had already withdrawn the no-curvature-to-detect claim.

**Stale VG13 window rationale (review §6.1, reporting portion) — confirmed.** The wrapper docstring and the report's age-restriction callout still gave the retired single-dataset-above-18-months justification; the definitions comment, [202608171500](202608171500-reporting-scope-audit.md) and the adopted `window-22` decision ([202608211100](202608211100-window-22-adopted.md)) supersede it. The VG13 sensitivity callout also said one variant was registered when four are (`single-admin`, `window-22`, `window-25`, `window-22-vague-anchors`), two of them fitted.

**Wrapper/graph mismatch (issue item 5) — confirmed, beyond the review's citations.** The VG11 and VG12 wrapper docstrings described "study-level random intercepts (non-centred parameterisation)"; the registered graphs sample the study block **centred** (`centred_study_re=True`, 2026-08-05) and set the child scale through the variance partition.

**Understood-count cast (review §6.5) — confirmed.** `build_model_re` cast `understood` with `dtype=int` before any finite/integral/range check; the spoken side gets all three from `nested_outcome_spec`.

**GP orthogonality overstatement (review §4.3) — confirmed, with one sharpening.** The re-anchoring shift `g - g[anchor]` restores a constant component, so the composite is not orthogonal to the full basis in the raw sense. The sharpening: orthogonality to `z` survives _exactly_ (not merely as slope-orthogonality in spirit), because `z` is standardised over the observed rows and therefore sums to zero there; for the tent basis, orthogonality to each hat holds only up to the shared constant. No identification failure follows — the constant direction is pinned by the anchor.

**Already addressed before this record (by #244, after the review's commit).** The leave-one-administration-out framing of LOO is now stated unconditionally in `render_loo_section`; the predictive-calibration section states its in-sample conditioning; the VG12 recovery failure (review §5.2) is recorded in the `build_variance_partition` docstring.

## 2. What changed

1. **TD source-level deduplication.** `load_data`'s typically-developing query now selects `DISTINCT *` from `wordbank_child` **before** the outcome projection, so exact full-row copies collapse while distinct same-child, same-age administrations that collide only after projection survive. Removal counts of record: 22 (VG11), 3 (VG12), 2 (VG13); frame sizes 18,522 → 18,500, 7,052 → 7,049, 6,358 → 6,356. Regression tests: a fixture test for the source-identity semantics and a real-database test pinning the frame sizes (`tests/test_data_utils.py`).
2. **Calibration frames use the registered language scope.** Both frame builders in `scripts/kappa_conditional_calibration.py` now pass `definition.td_languages`, mirroring the engines; a real-database test pins the calibration frames to the registered frames.
3. **Estimand corrections.** VG11/VG12 median-trend captions now describe the bands as predictive for individual children; the VG13 joint-intervals caption and the glossary's population-level entry describe the zero-effect curve as the typical (median) child rather than the average; the headline `kappa` row is labelled "Within-child spread between same-age administrations" whenever the fit carries a child effect.
4. **Stale prose removed.** The VG12 beyond-the-data callout is replaced by an accurate statement of the 25-month cap; the VG13 BFMI callout now describes E-BFMI as a global warning that cannot certify central estimates; the VG13 GP paragraph concludes curvature was not identified rather than that trajectories are straight; the VG13 window callout, sensitivity callout, wrapper docstring and `docs/models/README.md` entry now carry the current window rationale and the adopted `window-22` successor; VG11/VG12 wrapper docstrings match the registered centred-study-block graphs.
5. **Understood counts validated before casting.** `require_valid_counts` (finite, integral, in `[0, n_trials]`) runs before the integer cast in `common_bivariate_re`, mirroring the spoken side's `nested_outcome_spec` checks.
6. **GP orthogonality claims corrected.** `_orthogonalise_and_anchor` and the anchored-GP builder docstrings, and the two orthogonalisation test modules, now state the accurate contract: point-anchored, exactly orthogonal to `z` over the observed rows, centred-orthogonal to the remaining basis directions, level identified by the anchor rather than by orthogonality.

## 3. What this makes stale

The deduplication changes the VG11, VG12 and VG13 analysis frames (by 22, 3 and 2 rows), so their fits of record no longer reproduce from the current code. Per the completion criteria in #240, affected models and comparison artefacts should be **regenerated only after the graph-changing decisions in the remaining tier are settled** — in particular the child-loading sensitivity and the VG21 promotion — rather than refitted piecemeal for two-row changes. The calibration-scope fix changes no fit: it corrects the provenance of future calibration evidence. The review's §3.6 observation stands that the recorded evidence in [202608020829](202608020829-kappa-and-eta-q-prior-recalibration.md) and `docs/models/PRIORS.md` was computed on English-only frames; regenerating that evidence on the registered frames is now a matter of re-running the tooling, and is left with #240 so its interpretation happens with the study owner.
