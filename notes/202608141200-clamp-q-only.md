# The 84-month corner: `clamp_mean_above_hi_anchor` applies to two means, and only one needs it

> [!NOTE]
> Drafted by an LLM-based AI tool (Claude Code/Opus 5).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Adopted on 2026-08-14.** All eight DS joint models (VG05, VG07-VG10, VG14-VG16) now set `clamp_mean_above_hi_anchor = CLAMP_Q_ONLY` and are being refit. The sections below record the measurement that justified it; §7 records the adoption and what it changed.

## 1. The observation

VG10's posterior predictive trend for words spoken has a pronounced corner at 84 months. Expected vocabulary climbs steadily to 83 months and then very nearly stops.

| series                         | slope 78–83 mo | slope 85–90 mo |    ratio |
| ------------------------------ | -------------: | -------------: | -------: |
| **VG10 spoken** (both clamped) |  9.69 words/mo |  1.20 words/mo | **0.12** |
| VG01 spoken (no clamp)         |  4.30 words/mo |  5.12 words/mo |     1.19 |
| VG02 understood (no clamp)     |  3.51 words/mo |  3.15 words/mo |     0.90 |

VG01 and VG02 are the controls that make this diagnostic: **the same Down syndrome pool, the same `(24, 84)` slope anchors, and no clamp**. Neither shows anything at 84 months. VG13 also sets the flag `False` explicitly; all eight joint DS models (VG05, VG07–VG10, VG14–VG16) set it `True`.

84 months is not a developmental landmark. It is the high slope anchor — a modelling choice — and it sits inside the reported range, since spoken now reports to 90 months.

## 2. The mechanism, and why it compounds

`clamp_mean_above_hi_anchor` levels a trajectory's logit-linear mean off above the high anchor rather than extrapolating it to the top of the 115-month GP domain. Above the anchor the linear term stops contributing by construction, so all remaining growth must come from the GP — which is anchored at 54 months and has little to work with where spoken data thin out.

The flag was a **single boolean passed to both means**: the understood mean and the production ratio `q` (`common_bivariate.py:552`/`566`, `common_bivariate_re.py:546`/`562`, `common_trivariate.py:672`/`685`, `common_joint_modality.py:1121`/`1134`). Because spoken is `p_U(a) * q(a)`, the spoken trajectory inherits **two** levelled-off factors multiplied together. That is why its corner is sharper than either factor's alone.

## 3. The evidence: the saturation is `q`'s alone

The recorded justification in VG10's definition is about `q`: without the clamp "the fitted `q` mean alone reaches 0.993 at 115 mo (P(mean > 0.99) = 0.90) against a realised 0.842". It then adds that "understood shows the same defect about 3x milder", and that second claim is the one that does not survive measurement.

Extrapolating **VG10's own fitted anchors** past the clamp — the model of record's posterior, not a proxy — gives:

| quantity         | 84 mo | 100 mo |    115 mo | P(mean > 0.99) at 115 |
| ---------------- | ----: | -----: | --------: | --------------------: |
| understood `p_U` | 0.815 |  0.915 |     0.962 |             **0.000** |
| ratio `q`        | 0.943 |  0.985 | **0.996** |             **0.999** |

`q` saturates, with near-certainty of exceeding 0.99 — worse on the current data than when the clamp was added. The understood mean reaches 0.962 and **never crosses 0.99 in any posterior draw**.

The unclamped models agree, and they are fitted rather than extrapolated:

| unclamped model | p(115) | P(mean > 0.99) | realised max at ≥84 mo |
| --------------- | -----: | -------------: | ---------------------: |
| VG02 understood |  0.844 |          0.000 |         0.936 (n = 13) |
| VG01 spoken     |  0.612 |          0.000 |         0.826 (n = 59) |

VG02's understood mean reaches 0.844 at 115 months against a realised maximum of 0.936. That is not a runaway.

**Caveat, stated plainly.** VG02 is univariate — no subject random effects, no `q` decomposition — so it is a control on the _pool and anchors_, not on VG10's structure. The decisive evidence is the VG10 extrapolation in the first table; VG02 corroborates it.

## 4. What clamping `q` only would do

Above 84 months, spoken would grow at roughly `q(84) x dp_U/da` = 0.943 x 5.06 ≈ **4.8 words/mo**, against 9.69 below. A ratio near 0.5 rather than the present 0.12: a real developmental slowdown remains, and the artificial corner largely goes.

This is a prediction, not a result. The GP will move once the mean's shape changes, so the variant has to be fitted.

## 5. Implementation: why a string, not a second field

`clamp_mean_above_hi_anchor` now accepts `True`, `False`, or `CLAMP_Q_ONLY` (`"q_only"`), resolved by `definitions.clamp_targets()` into `(clamp_understood, clamp_q)`.

A second boolean field would have been the obvious design and is the wrong one here. `fit_manifest.json` fingerprints the model definition with `asdict` and compares it as **whole-object equality**, so a new field adds a key to all fifteen models' definitions and invalidates every model of record at once — including VG13, mid-fit at the time. Widening this field's domain leaves `True`/`False` serialising exactly as before, so only a definition that actually opts in changes. Verified: all twelve fitted models remained `[valid]` for `--purpose publish` after the change.

Two traps are pinned by `tests/test_clamp_scope.py`:

- **`"q_only"` is truthy.** `if definition.clamp_mean_above_hi_anchor:` clamps both means, silently doing the opposite of what was asked. Engines must resolve through `clamp_targets`.
- **No model of record may serialise the clamp as a non-boolean**, or its fingerprint moves and it needs a refit.

## 6. Status and what would settle it

Registered as `("vg10", "clamp-q-only")` and fitted as a sensitivity; it is not adopted. The comparison to read is whether the understood trajectory below 84 months — where the data are — moves at all. If it does not, clamping `p_U` was doing no necessary work and the flag should become `q`-only for the family, at the cost of a refit of eight DS models. Note that **VG08 sits 0.0015 from the R-hat gate** at the strongest tuning available, so any family-wide refit is a coin-flip on it.

Two related questions were considered and left alone. Moving the high anchor to 90 or 96 months was rejected: the anchor is shared between the two means, and above 84 understood has only 13 rows from 12 children against spoken's 59, so no single position serves both. Widening `_CLAMP_SOFTNESS` (currently 50, giving a transition over roughly 0.8 months) would round the corner without addressing which means are clamped, and eats into the region where `p_slope_hi` is interpretable.

## 7. Adoption

The variant fitted cleanly (gate PASS, 0 divergences, max R-hat 1.0049) and `compare_sensitivity.py` returned **robust: all 395 quantities within the baseline's 89% interval**. That is the result that decided it — freeing `p_U` above the anchor changes nothing beyond what the baseline's own interval already admits, so clamping it was doing no necessary work.

What the fitted variant showed, against the model of record:

| quantity                         | baseline | clamp-q-only | change |
| -------------------------------- | -------: | -----------: | -----: |
| understood at 24 mo              |    113.6 |        113.8 |  +0.2% |
| understood at 48 mo              |    309.9 |        309.7 |  −0.0% |
| understood at 72 mo              |    463.1 |        464.8 |  +0.4% |
| understood at 84 mo              |    542.3 |        527.7 |  −2.7% |
| spoken at 72 mo                  |    342.1 |        344.4 |  +0.7% |
| spoken at 90 mo                  |    449.6 |        452.9 |  +0.7% |
| spoken slope ratio 85–90 / 78–83 |     0.12 |         0.49 |      — |

Below 72 months — where the data are — nothing moves by more than 0.7%. The corner softens from a slope ratio of 0.12 to 0.49: a real developmental slowdown remains, which is right, but the artificial cliff goes.

So this is a **presentation correction, not an inferential one**. It removes an artefact that reads as a finding about vocabulary at exactly seven years, without moving any estimate the report quotes. That is also why it was safe to adopt family-wide rather than piecemeal.

Adopted on all eight models that carried the flag, not only the two that needed refitting for other reasons: a mixed family would render the same trajectory differently across reports, which is worse than either consistent choice.

**The variant was inverted rather than retired.** `("vg10", "clamp-q-only")` is now `("vg10", "clamp-both")`, restoring the old behaviour, so the decision keeps a check that can still fail. A registered variant that can no longer vary anything reads as robustness it has not demonstrated — the principle the retired ceiling and `sign-include-uk06` variants went out under.

**Cost.** A refit of eight DS models. VG08 sits 0.0015 from the R-hat gate at the strongest tuning available, so its re-convergence was a known coin-flip going in.
