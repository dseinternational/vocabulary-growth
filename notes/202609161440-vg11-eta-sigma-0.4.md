# VG11 adopts the standard GP amplitude prior, `eta_sigma` 0.4

> [!NOTE]
> Drafted by an LLM-based AI tool (Claude Code/Opus 5).

**Date:** 2026-09-16. **Decision, by the study owner, the same day ([#357](https://github.com/dseinternational/vocabulary-growth/issues/357)):** narrow VG11's GP amplitude prior from `HalfNormal(0.5)` to `HalfNormal(0.4)` and refit the nine publication models from the resulting commit. **What this note is:** the evidence for the change, what moved in the code, and what the change does not establish. It follows [`202609160500`](202609160500-vg11-length-scale-exception.md), which recorded the convergence exception and left the model alone.

## 1. Why

VG11's model of record failed the hard R-hat gate at `eta_sigma` 0.5 twice, on the same ridge. In August it failed at 1.0125 on one HSGP basis coefficient, `g_unit_hsgp_coeffs[4]`. On 2026-09-16 it failed at 1.0116 on `ell`/`ell_unit`. Both times every reported quantity converged with margin. [`202609160500`](202609160500-vg11-length-scale-exception.md) §2 traces this to the GP's amplitude/length-scale ridge. The GP is orthogonalised against the constant and the line, and every length scale the prior admits leaves nearly the same residual shape, so the data identify how much curvature there is but not how it splits between `eta` and `ell`.

The registered `vg11 / eta-narrow` arm had already measured the remedy at `rep` on 2026-08-15 ([`202608142000`](202608142000-refit-run-record-and-disk-failure.md) §5c):

|                            | `eta_sigma` 0.5 (model of record) | `eta_sigma` 0.4 (`eta-narrow`) |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------: | -----------------------------: |
| divergences                |                                16 |                          **3** |
| max R-hat (all parameters) |                    1.0125 — fails |                1.0075 — passes |
| min ESS                    |                             1,139 |                            863 |
| fitted `eta`               |                             1.027 |                          0.901 |

All eight reporting ages agreed to within 0.22%, every point lay inside the 0.5 fit's own 89% interval, and the largest difference was 0.44 words at 30 months on a count of 430. The sensitivity harness scored it robust, with a maximum absolute delta of 0.437 words (`docs/models/PRIORS.md`, "Sensitivity results").

Narrowing the prior is the substantive fix. The exception is a disclosure, and it stays one only if VG11 still trips.

## 2. What changed

- **`definitions.py`:** `VG11.eta_sigma` 0.5 → 0.4, with the decision, the evidence and the date in a comment. VG11's comment no longer says it shares a widened `eta` with VG03. VG03 keeps 0.5, and so does VG12.
- **`sensitivity/registry.py`:** `("vg11", "eta-narrow")` at 0.4 would have become a no-op. It is replaced by `("vg11", "eta-wide")` at 0.5, which keeps the former model of record reachable as a one-change sensitivity. The registered arm count stays 117. VG12's own `eta-narrow` is untouched.
- **`docs/models/PRIORS.md`:** the Target 8 row names `eta-wide`. The sensitivity-results row keeps its `eta-narrow` label, because it is the evidence for this decision, with a footnote saying what it was scored against and that the arm is now registered the other way round.
- **`docs/models/vg11/index.qmd`:** the registered-variants list and the GP-amplitude paragraph name the change.
- **`tests/support/graph_baseline.json` and `graph_reference_points.json`:** regenerated for `vg11` only. Only `vg11`'s log-density moved: −2001.664 → −2002.040 at the fixed point. No other model's key and no free-variable set changed.
- **`CONVERGENCE_EXCEPTIONS["VG11"]` is left as it is.** `fit_artifacts.py` is in the hashed package, so removing the entry would restale the fits again. Whether it can come out is decided after the refit, from the refit's own diagnostics.

The change moves VG11's definition fingerprint and the package's executable-code signature. The four Down syndrome fits promoted from `5a05780` on 2026-09-16 are stale, as are VG12, VG23, VG21 and VG26 from `82103df` and VG11's 2026-09-08 model of record. All nine refit from the merge commit.

## 3. What it does not establish

- **That the refit will pass the gate.** The 0.4 measurement is one fit, made on the frame and graph of 2026-08-15. Since then VG11 has gained sex as a covariate (#324), and its ages became complete months (#352). A pass at 1.0075 is not a margin that survives every change of frame. If VG11 still trips, the exception discloses it as before.
- **That the amplitude is now calibrated.** The data ask for more amplitude than either prior offers. Under 0.5 the fitted `eta` (1.027) sat at prior CDF 0.960. Under 0.4 it sat at 0.901, at CDF **0.976**. Narrowing moves the posterior further into the prior's tail. This is regularisation of a smoothing hyperparameter the report does not interpret, and it is what bought the mixing. It is not a better-calibrated prior, and [`202608050900`](202608050900-td-hierarchical-geometry.md) §5's calibration finding for this family stands.
- **That the DS-versus-TD spread contrast is unchanged.** `tau_subject` was 1.039 under both priors in August, but the contrast has never been checked through `comparison.py`. That is #357's outstanding check. It is run after the refit, against the `eta_sigma` 0.5 trace retained at `failed/VG11-age-spoken-td-re-20260916T034535Z/` (same data and graph, one prior apart), on the `TD_KEYS` path of `scripts/compare_ds_td_re.py`, and reported before anything is published.
- **Anything about VG12 or VG03.** Their amplitude priors are separate decisions. VG12's own `eta-narrow` moved its amplitude posterior visibly while its trajectories stayed inside the interval (`PRIORS.md`).
