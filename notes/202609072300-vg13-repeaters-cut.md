# Pinning VG13's child scales repairs the geometry — and that is why the cut is not the remedy

> [!NOTE]
> Drafted by an LLM-based AI tool (Claude Code/Opus 5).

Date: 2026-09-07. Six `test`-tier fits of VG13 on the project's Windows workstation, about two and a half hours of compute, plus a second seed of four of them. The check that [202609072230](202609072230-vg12-repeaters-prior.md) named as outstanding for [#289](https://github.com/dseinternational/vocabulary-growth/issues/289) task 4.6, run the same day. It answers that question — and a control arm added to make the answer interpretable turns out to change what task 4.6 should be.

## The question

[202609072200](202609072200-vg08-replication-thinning.md) established that the typically developing (TD) models' low energy BFMI and their τ–κ ridge are what a child effect looks like when most children are seen once. [202609072230](202609072230-vg12-repeaters-prior.md) then established, on VG12, that a repeaters-calibrated _prior_ cannot repair it but a _cut_ — holding the between-child / within-child split at the repeaters' estimate — can, taking VG12's min BFMI from 0.208 to 0.589. That note was explicit about what it had not tested:

> VG13, VG21 and VG23 have two free child scales and no partition. The same cut is to fix `tau_subj_u` and `tau_subj_q` from a repeaters-only fit, or to add the partition first. **This has not been tested**; the analogue of this experiment on VG13 is the next check, and it is bivariate, so about twice the cost.

VG13 is the right model to run it on: VG23 is VG13's frame _exactly_ plus one free correlation, and VG21 is VG13 with the window widened to 8–22 months. Measured on their own loader paths, the three share one replication profile — 828, 828 and 943 replicated children out of 5,496, 5,496 and 5,707, all drawn from the same three of six studies (Thal, Floccia, ByersHeinlein). One answer covers the trio.

One structural difference from VG12 makes the test necessary rather than a formality. VG12 carries `subject_variance_partition`, so its split is a single `Beta` share and a near-degenerate Beta pins it. VG13 carries two independent `HalfNormal` scales, and a `HalfNormal` has its mode at zero — there is **no** member of that family expressing "the scale is about 0.73". So on VG13 the prior arm of the VG12 experiment does not exist, and the pin is the only lever there is.

## What was fitted

`scripts/experiments/vg13_repeaters_cut.py`, four arms, all VG13 as registered except where stated, all at `test` (4 chains × 2,000 draws after 2,000 tuning, target accept 0.9), fitted through the bivariate RE engine's own stages on a supplied frame:

- **baseline** — the registered analysis frame: 6,356 rows, 5,496 children, six studies, 15.1% repeaters.
- **repeaters** — only the children with two or more visits: 1,688 rows, 828 children, and **three** of the six studies. Study and child codes re-issued densely.
- **pinned** — the full frame with `tau_subj_u` and `tau_subj_q` held at the repeaters arm's posterior means, near-degenerately (sd 0.001, the VG12 precedent: the dimension still enters the energy, so the BFMI stays comparable).
- **pinned-baseline** — the control, added before the pinned arms ran. The same pin, at the **baseline's own** posterior means. Circular as an estimator, and that is the point: it separates two things the `pinned` arm confounds — whether the geometry is repaired by _removing the split direction_, or by _the repeaters' information specifically_. Without it a BFMI rise in `pinned` cannot be attributed.

The pin is applied by intercepting the two `pm.HalfNormal` calls by name and substituting a `TruncatedNormal`; every other `HalfNormal` in the graph passes through. That this does exactly what it claims — the two named scales move, the free-RV set is unchanged, nothing else in the graph differs, and the patch reverts — was verified on the built graph before any of these fits were run.

The baseline reproduces the `rep` fit of record's geometry — min BFMI 0.240 against 0.248, ridge +0.581 against +0.576, energy ratio 2.76 against 2.81, `tau_subj_u` 0.7348 against 0.734 and `tau_subj_q` 1.1076 against 1.108 — so the comparison is like for like, by the same standard the VG12 experiment used.

### The diagnosis generalises to the two-scale models

The repeaters arm's min BFMI is 0.449 against the baseline's 0.240, and both ridges collapse (+0.581 → +0.172, +0.444 → +0.020). That is VG12's repeaters arm (0.510, +0.255) reproduced on a bivariate model with no partition. So [#229](https://github.com/dseinternational/vocabulary-growth/issues/229)'s reframe holds for VG13: the pathology is what a child effect looks like without replication, and it is not an artefact of the partition — which was already known, since VG13 has the ridge and no partition, but it is now known in the direction that matters, that replication is what removes it.

### The transplant is not safe

The repeaters do not merely split the variance differently. They have **less of it, on both components**:

| quantity | baseline        | repeaters       | difference |    rel | z (combined SE) |
| -------- | --------------- | --------------- | ---------: | -----: | --------------: |
| τ_u      | 0.7348 ± 0.0134 | 0.6116 ± 0.0180 |    −0.1231 | −16.8% |           −5.49 |
| τ_q      | 1.1076 ± 0.0192 | 0.9202 ± 0.0297 |    −0.1875 | −16.9% |           −5.30 |
| κ_u      | 39.63 ± 2.51    | 47.31 ± 4.08    |      +7.68 | +19.4% |           +1.60 |
| κ_s      | 34.15 ± 2.97    | 47.47 ± 7.21    |     +13.32 | +39.0% |           +1.71 |

κ is a concentration, so a _higher_ κ is _less_ within-child dispersion. Between-child variance falls to 69% of the baseline's and within-child variance to 84%. Converting κ to a logit-scale variance at a fixed reference proportion `p0` (as `build_variance_partition` does) and totalling, the repeaters' **total** scatter is 23.5%, 25.9% and 27.4% below the baseline's at `p0` = 0.05, 0.1041 and 0.20 — robust to the conversion, which the split is not.

So the three repeater studies are not a corrected version of the pool. They are a **less variable subpopulation**, and transplanting an absolute logit-scale quantity from them imports that. This is the caveat 202609072230 raised for VG12 — "transplanting their share to the whole frame assumes the between/within proportion travels even though the total does not" — with more force, because VG12 transplants a dimensionless share (0.598 → 0.633, about 6%) while VG13 would transplant absolute scales (−17%, at z ≈ −5.4).

What the pinned arm then does is the confirmation. Its κ is **27.47**, nowhere near the repeaters' 47.31. The pin does not transplant the repeaters' variance decomposition at all: it takes the full pool's larger total variance, holds the between-child part at a less variable subsample's level, and the excess lands in within-child dispersion, inflating it by ×1.44 on understood and ×1.52 on production. That is the failure mode #229 already records as closed for a neighbouring proposal — "the variance does not disappear, it migrates into `kappa`, which _is_ a reported estimand … it would recreate the `DISP_DS_KEY` defect in mirror image" — in a partial form, and κ is a cross-population reported quantity.

### The control is the finding

`pinned-baseline` clears the threshold too: min BFMI **0.507** against the baseline's 0.240, both ridges gone (+0.038, +0.045), energy ratio 1.92 — and κ_u 39.56 against 39.63, κ_s 34.17 against 34.15, that is, **every reported quantity unchanged**. It is also the only arm that passes the ≤ 1.01 R̂ gate at _both_ seeds (1.0073 and 1.0082, against the baseline's 1.0162 and 1.0110); `pinned` passes at one of the two.

So the geometry repair does not need the repeaters' information. It does not need _any_ information. It needs only the removal of the split direction, and pinning at **any** value removes it.

That is worth stating plainly, because it undercuts the premise task 4.6 is written on:

> **A clearing BFMI is not evidence that the intervals became reliable.** It can be manufactured by freezing the split at an arbitrary value while adding nothing. In `pinned-baseline` the posterior sd of τ_u falls from 0.0134 to 0.001 — so the reported interval becomes _narrower_, and less honest, while the diagnostic that currently warns a reader goes quiet.

The low BFMI in the TD models is a symptom of a real identification weakness: the split rests on the 15% of children with replication, and the rest is supplied by the Beta-Binomial's functional form. Pinning treats the symptom. On these data it either changes nothing (pinned at the model's own answer) or moves two reported estimands substantially in a direction driven by three studies (pinned at the repeaters').

## What it means for task 4.6

**The remedy for the bivariate TD trio is not a reparameterisation, and #289's sequencing premise does not hold.** #289 states that the TD set needs a refit "after task 4.6, whose remedy is a reparameterisation that would invalidate one done now". On this evidence there is no reparameterisation worth making:

1. Pinning at the repeaters' values is disqualified — it moves τ by −17% and inflates within-child dispersion by about 50%, on a three-study basis whose total scatter is a quarter below the pool's.
2. Pinning at the model's own values is free but empty — it silences the diagnostic without adding information and narrows the interval it should widen.
3. Adding the partition first, the alternative 202609072230 names, is available but is not a small change: `subject_variance_partition` is a field on `UnivariateREModelDefinition` only, so giving it to VG13, VG21 and VG23 means a new field on `BivariateModelDefinition`, invalidating every bivariate fit including the Down syndrome ones — mechanically fine through a `BACKFILL_DEFAULTS` entry defaulting to `None`, whose claim is trivially checkable, but it leaves the statistical question open, because a bivariate model has two child scales and two κ blocks and "the budget" is therefore not well defined without deciding whether it is one shared budget or two.

What is left is what #229 already lists: **option 4**, report total scatter at matched age and stop decomposing it, which #229 records as needing no refit and which the `p0`-robustness above supports as the better-behaved quantity; or **option 6**, weight data admission toward longitudinal sources, the only option that supplies the missing information. Both are decisions rather than code, and neither invalidates a fit.

### What this does and does not say about VG11 and VG12

The control arm identifies a **mechanism** — the energy pathology is the split direction, and removing it repairs the BFMI wherever the split is held. That mechanism is not specific to two free scales: a near-degenerate `Beta` on `subject_variance_share` removes the same direction. So it **predicts** that a `pinned-baseline` control on VG12 would also clear the threshold, and therefore that VG12's 0.589 is not by itself evidence that the repeaters' share is the right value.

That is a prediction, not a result. **The VG12 control has not been run**, and it is the cheap test that would settle it: one `test`-tier VG12 arm pinned at its own share posterior mean, against the 0.589 already recorded. Until it has, 202609072230's recommendation for VG11 and VG12 stands on its own terms, and two things genuinely distinguish those models from the trio:

- they transplant a **dimensionless share** (0.598 → 0.633, about 6%) rather than absolute scales, so the composition objection that disqualifies the transplant here is much weaker; and
- propagating the repeaters' uncertainty **widens** τ_TD to about 0.70 ± 0.02 against a fit-of-record 0.686 ± 0.014, which is a real gain in honesty rather than the interval collapse that pinning at a point produces.

VG11 is further out still: its repeaters span **six of ten** studies against the trio's three of six, so the "less variable subpopulation" objection may not apply to it at all. None of that was measured here.

**The practical consequence is that the TD refit is unblocked on the registered graphs**, with one residual risk stated rather than hidden. For the bivariate trio it is unblocked outright, on the evidence above. For VG11 and VG12 it is unblocked _conditionally_: if the share cut is later adopted for them it would be a definition change, and fits made now would be staled by it and need redoing. That risk is worth taking — it is two models rather than seven, the cut is not yet decided (it needs the VG12 control above and an owner call on #229), and the alternative is worse, because the output root currently holds **no fits at all**, so nothing renders and the eighteen models that are not VG11 or VG12 would wait on a decision that does not concern them.

The caveat that these fits' interval bounds are less reliable than their point estimates should travel with them — but as the honest statement of a thin-replication design, not as a defect awaiting a reparameterisation this experiment finds no case for.

## Caveats

- **`test` tier, and VG13 does not converge at it.** The baseline misses the gate at max R̂ 1.0162 / min ESS 355. This is the documented expectation rather than a new defect — `docs/runbooks/full-refit.md` prescribes `rep-hightune` for VG11/VG12/VG13, with `target_accept 0.99` for VG13 "which also has divergences". The arms are comparable to each other because they are fitted identically and the baseline reproduces the fit of record's geometry, but **no arm here is a converged fit and none of these numbers is publishable**.
- **The repeaters arm is a poor calibration source in its own right**: 47 divergences against the baseline's 2, and min ESS 237. It is a worse fit than the thing it would be calibrating.
- **`p0` is assumed, not registered.** VG13 carries no partition and therefore no reference proportion, so the total-scatter arithmetic above is shown across a range rather than at a fitted value. The −25% repeaters gap is robust across that range; the _share_ is not — it moves from 0.50 to 0.77 across `p0` = 0.05 to 0.20 on the baseline alone, which is itself a caution about quoting a share for a model that does not parameterise one.
- **One model.** VG13 is the bivariate TD model with the identical-frame sibling; VG21 differs in window and VG23 in one free correlation, and neither was fitted here. VG11 is **not** covered and should not be assumed to behave the same way: its repeaters span six of ten studies against VG13's three of six, so the composition objection that disqualifies the transplant here is materially weaker there.
- **Not fits of record; nothing here is publishable.**
