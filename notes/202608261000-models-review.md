## 4. The plan

1. **Refit all.** The forthcoming refit run covers every registered model, retirement candidates included, so each candidate's archived record is produced by the same run, on the same data, under the same convergence and provenance gates as the models that stay. No model is retired against a stale fit.
2. **Archive the candidates' final fits** before any registry change. The natural mechanism is the existing `--upload` AzCopy route (`DSERESEARCH_BLOB_CONTAINER_URL`), to a location to be settled — it should be distinct from the live published container so an archived fit cannot be mistaken for a current one. What must survive as the minimum record: `fit_manifest.json`, the posterior summary tables, `loo_summary.csv` (it feeds the frozen lineage table), the diagnostics/caveats artefacts and the figures. Whether the trace travels at `full` or `compact` is a disk decision (`--trace-persistence`), noting that a compacted trace forecloses later LOSO or recomputation for that model.
3. **Freeze the report sections that read the retired models live.** The inventory's rule is that a superseded model never supplies a number in the findings, and the mirror of that rule here is that a _retired_ model cannot keep supplying a live table. The lineage ELPD section and the signing chapter's VG14 contrast must become recorded comparison artefacts — dated, provenance-stamped, written once from the archived fits — before the registry entries are removed, or those sections silently lose their inputs and render as "pending".
4. **Retire**, which is the registration checklist of `docs/models/README.md` run in reverse: `MODEL_REGISTRY` and the `model_vgNN.py` module; the pinned test lists (the kappa-form lists in `test_model_definitions.py`, `CLAMP_Q_ONLY_MODELS` in `test_clamp_scope.py`, the engine dispatch in `test_trend_gp_consolidation.py`); `ENGINE_BY_MODEL` in `scripts/regenerate_plots.py`; and the inventory plus the three synced instruction files. Inventory rows are retained VG06-style — marked retired with the reason and the archive location, not deleted.

## 5. What this buys

If the full candidate slate retires — VG01–VG04, VG05/VG07–VG09, VG14, and VG16 if #242 resolves to retirement — the refit fleet drops from twenty models to about ten on every future cycle, roughly halving refit compute, trace disk and the validation surface, with the findings chapters untouched and only the methods-history sections changing how they are sourced.
