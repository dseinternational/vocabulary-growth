# August model-retirement proposal

> [!NOTE]
> Drafted with assistance from Claude Code/Fable 5. Revised with assistance from OpenAI Codex/GPT-6 on 7 October 2026.

The 26 August 2026 review proposed refitting all twenty models then registered, archiving selected development fits and retiring models no longer used in reporting. Its dependency inventory described the report chapters and publication checks at that revision.

The [9 September role decision](202609091600-model-roles-settled.md) replaced that plan. The development models remain registered, but the default refit scope selects reporting models and unclassified candidates. Publication can skip invalid development and superseded fits. It still requires models of record, TD references and unclassified candidates to pass their checks. This resolves the earlier concern that every stale development fit could block publication.

Use the [current inventory](../docs/models/README.md#model-roles) and [refit scope](../docs/runbooks/full-refit.md#which-models-a-run-covers) for model selection. The original retirement list, report-dependency table and execution steps remain in Git history; they are not instructions for the current registry.
