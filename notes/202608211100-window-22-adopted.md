# `window-22` is the defensible extension of the matched-comprehension comparison

> [!NOTE]
> Drafted by an LLM-based AI tool (Claude Code/Opus 5).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Decision record, 2026-08-21, taken by the study owner on the evidence below. Both extended-window variants were fitted 2026-08-20 at VG13's own baseline sampling, so the window is the only factor separating them from the model of record and from each other. `window-22` is adopted; `window-25` is rejected as ceiling-contaminated. Every matched-comprehension contrast is now capped at **320 words understood** (`compare_ds_td_re.MAX_MATCHED_U`). §6 records what is still outstanding: the variant is not a registered model, so nothing published yet rests on it.

## 1. What was fitted

Follow-up 1 of [`202608171500`](202608171500-reporting-scope-audit.md) asked for both windows, because the extension's value and its risk come from the same rows. Both fits used tune 12000 / draws 8000 / target_accept 0.99 / 6 chains — VG13's own `rep` settings — so no part of the difference is the sampler's.

| window                | rows / children | max U reached | div | max R-hat | min ESS | BFMI min | hard gate |
| --------------------- | --------------- | ------------: | --: | --------: | ------: | -------: | --------- |
| VG13 (8–18, baseline) | 6,358 / 5,496   |         220.9 |   0 |   1.00538 |    1247 |    0.242 | PASS      |
| `window-22` (8–22)    | 6,786 / 5,707   |     **328.0** |   0 |   1.00682 |    1205 |    0.273 | PASS      |
| `window-25` (8–25)    | 7,052 / 5,819   |         355.8 |   0 |   1.00480 |    1101 |    0.255 | PASS      |

**Convergence does not discriminate.** All three clear R-hat ≤ 1.01 and ESS ≥ 400 with zero divergences, and all three carry the same energy-BFMI soft caveat VG13 already discloses. Both extensions are _better_ on BFMI than the baseline, so the caveat is not an argument against widening the window — the narrow window has it too.

## 2. The ceiling separates them, exactly where predicted

The audit predicted contamination from the Oxford CDI's 418-item cap, concentrated in the top three months (share of administrations within 10% of the form's ceiling: 7–8% at 19–22 months, then **20.2%, 27.7%, 36.1%** at 23, 24, 25). The fitted `q`-by-understood grids show it:

| understood | VG13 8–18 |            `window-25` |            `window-22` |
| ---------: | --------: | ---------------------: | ---------------------: |
|        100 |     0.098 |                  0.092 |                  0.094 |
|        200 |     0.206 |                  0.217 |                  0.219 |
|        250 |         — |                  0.319 |                  0.309 |
|        300 |         — |              **0.475** |              **0.426** |
|        328 |         — | **0.602** [0.58, 0.62] | **0.504** [0.47, 0.54] |

Below ~250 words the three agree within noise. Above it they separate, and by 328 words the 89% intervals no longer overlap. The direction is diagnostic rather than merely different: compressing _understood_ at the ceiling while spoken keeps rising inflates `q = S/U`, so a window that admits ceiling-saturated rows should read **high** — and `window-25` does, by 0.098 at 328 words. That is what contamination looks like; a real steepening would not be confined to the months where the instrument runs out of items.

## 3. The contrast the two windows support is not the same contrast

This is the finding that makes the pair worth the two fits, and it would have been invisible from either alone. DS `VG20` against each TD window, Δq = q_TD − q_DS:

| understood | `window-22` Δq           | P(TD>DS) | `window-25` Δq           | P(TD>DS) |
| ---------: | ------------------------ | -------: | ------------------------ | -------: |
|        175 | +0.09 [+0.07, +0.11]     |     1.00 | +0.09 [+0.06, +0.11]     |     1.00 |
|        200 | +0.09 [+0.04, +0.12]     |     1.00 | +0.09 [+0.04, +0.12]     |     1.00 |
|        250 | +0.03 [−0.05, +0.09]     |     0.71 | +0.03 [−0.04, +0.10]     |     0.78 |
|        300 | **−0.00** [−0.08, +0.07] |     0.49 | +0.05 [−0.03, +0.12]     |     0.83 |
|        320 | **+0.00** [−0.08, +0.08] |     0.51 | **+0.09** [+0.01, +0.17] |     0.96 |

**Under `window-22` the gap closes.** It peaks at +0.09 around 175–200 words and is indistinguishable from zero by 300 (P(TD>DS) = 0.49) and at 320 (0.51). **Under `window-25` the gap reopens** — +0.09 at 320 with P = 0.96 and an interval excluding zero.

Those are different scientific claims. One says children with Down syndrome converge on the typical production ratio once they understand enough words; the other says the disadvantage returns at high vocabulary. The second is an artefact of the ceiling, by §2. Fitting one window would have produced a publishable-looking answer with no way to tell which one it was.

## 4. What the adopted window changes for the report

The matched-comprehension comparison ran to 220.9 words and could only report the gap "narrowing to uncertain by ~200". It now runs to 320 and supports a positive statement: **the gap peaks near 175–200 words understood at about +0.09, then closes, and is statistically indistinguishable from zero from roughly 300 words onward.** That is a stronger and more useful finding than the one it replaces, and it is the shape the earlier small-sample literature suggested without being able to demonstrate.

## 5. The 320-word cap

`compare_ds_td_re.MAX_MATCHED_U = 320.0`, applied to every comprehension-matched contrast. It is set by the **TD side's ceiling-safe support**, not by the DS side and not by taste: `window-22` reaches 328.0, and 320 is the last grid level comfortably inside it. The DS side is not the binding constraint anywhere here — VG20's own range runs to 527.7 — so the top of the Down syndrome range still has no matched comparator, and that limitation is unchanged by this decision.

## 6. What this does _not_ settle

- **`window-22` is a registered sensitivity variant, not a registered model.** Follow-up 4 of the audit is explicit that a headline comparison should not rest on a sensitivity entry. The canonical comparison outputs in `output/comparisons/` were therefore regenerated from the **baseline VG13** after this analysis, and stop at 220.9 as before. Publishing the extension needs the variant promoted to a numbered model (VG21 — `VG17`/`VG18` are the sign-group prototypes and `VG19` is reserved by the child-slope plan) with all six obligations in `docs/models/README.md` §"Registering a new model".
- **Both variants' high-anchor priors come from in-sample medians, not published norms**, because no CDI comprehension norm exists above 18 months. That is weaker footing than the baseline window's priors have, and it is unresolved by these fits.
- **The kappa anchor magnitudes are inherited from VG12 rather than recalibrated** for the wider window.
- **`window-25` is not withdrawn as a fit.** It is the measurement of the ceiling exposure, and §2 and §3 are its result. It should not supply a reported number.
