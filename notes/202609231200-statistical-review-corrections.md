## Reading the historical notes

Dated correction notices point here from affected notes. They preserve the original record while withdrawing claims that the review found unsupported. In particular, the earlier claims of age-invariant signing association, a guaranteed quadrature error direction, an oracle hierarchy slope, and measurement-error bounds must not be used as current methodological support.

The broader prose corrections distinguish a zero-sum study constraint from equal data influence, separate calibration from independent validation, and withdraw causal or equivalence claims unsupported by observational comparisons. They also distinguish a fixed likelihood equation from its posterior after refitting shared parameters. Correction notices appear on 73 historical notes. The pre-specification has a clarification in its designated log; its registered estimand, threshold and decision rule are unchanged.

An odds ratio below one does not mean that most signed words are unspoken. With cells `(neither, sign only, speech only, both) = (4, 6, 56, 34)`, the odds ratio is about 0.405 while 85% of signed words are also spoken. Independence also permits overlap; it is not the absence of shared words.

A positive-scale equal-tailed interval excluding zero is not a test of nonzero variation. A continuous HalfNormal prior already gives zero probability to exactly zero and has a positive lower equal-tailed endpoint. Read posterior mass near a scientifically small scale, prior-to-posterior change, predictive consequences and sensitivity instead.

Source verification and descriptive audits can be rerun on the prepared data. These checks establish behaviour of the corrected implementation; they do not establish calibration of a production posterior.

Regenerate affected predictions and comparisons from compatible fits. Refit when the definition, prepared frame or enforced implementation identity requires it. Do not relabel old outputs as current results. Use the [full-refit runbook](../docs/runbooks/full-refit.md) and [recovery runbook](../docs/runbooks/parameter-recovery.md), including convergence checks and enough independent replicates for the claim being made.

## Reviewer follow-up on 24 September 2026

The follow-up addresses the comments on PR #367. Resume now has an explicit, recorded option for reviewed code-only changes. It still checks the definition, prepared frame and numerical libraries. Publication checks the original sampling checkout and the current reporting checkout. Recovery uses the documented definition backfills instead of raw dictionary equality.

Comparison publication accepts the selected reporting configuration and the existing caveat policy. A registered R-hat exception requires its matching recorded decision; failed effective sample size checks remain a barrier. The script displays accepted caveats for disclosure in the report. Comparison signatures follow each generator's local static imports instead of every script, using the package's common prose filter. Exploratory age-at-count CSVs now go into a nested directory outside the comparison publication inputs.

Trajectory loaders apply reporting age limits before weighted attainment, count-based comparisons and signing comparisons. An analysis combining quantities stops at their tightest limit; a speech-only weighted trajectory keeps its own limit. LOSO scoring prepares the same paired-count rule as the model engine once per frame. Observed speech above comprehension uses the marginal fallback rather than an impossible conditional count.

The ICC bootstrap now repeats the point estimator's complete procedure. It resamples all children, refits the age and study adjustment, then selects repeated observations for the variance calculation. A fixed resample reproduces the point estimate. This check does not establish confidence interval coverage.

Conditional predictions record importance-weight effective sample size, calculated as one divided by the sum of squared normalised weights. They warn below 100. This is a screening threshold for concentration in the candidate weights, not an MCMC effective sample size or a guarantee of interval coverage. Low values call for more candidates and a numerical stability check. Within-child prediction interpolates the curves once for each visit, rather than repeating that calculation for each child and chunk. The expressive comparison self-check now exercises the nested predictive distribution, including its discrete zero mass.

The final combined fast and slow suite passed 2,950 tests with 58 skips. This includes a repeated-resume test that distinguishes original sampling provenance from intermediate reporting runs. Ruff, mypy, the notes index, Markdown formatting and spelling passed. The standalone expressive self-check passed. The test logs retain compiler, plotting and low-weight-ESS warnings. No production posterior, recovery campaign or published report was regenerated during this follow-up.
