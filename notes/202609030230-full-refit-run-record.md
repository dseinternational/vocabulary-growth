## Parameter recovery

**The tier is the limiting factor, not the models.** The harness assesses a replicate only if max R-hat is at or below 1.01 and minimum ESS at or above 400, and carries divergences as caveats. On that rule three of nine were assessed: VG20 one of three (R-hat 1.0116 / 1.0053 / 1.0136), VG12 two of three (1.0060 / 1.0064 / 1.0114, with 17 / 13 / 18 divergences), VG15 none of three (1.0152 / 1.0176 / 1.0198, and no divergences at all).
