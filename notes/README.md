Working notes are dated records, named `YYYYMMDDHHMM-slug.md`, and reflect the repository and its figures **as of their date** — later data-rule or model changes are not back-ported into them.

Two maintenance modes, adopted 2026-07-26. Notes that record **decisions about data or analyses** are preserved as written, with corrections appended as flagged blocks — there the trail is evidence for why a rule or number stands. Notes that carry an **argument or proposal** may be consolidated into a successor when correction strata start to obscure the current position; the superseded files are deleted (their full history stays in git and the pull-request threads) and the supersession is recorded here. Frozen documents — pre-registrations — change only through their own deviation log.
