# Vocabulary data - UK (5)

> [!NOTE]
> Revised with assistance from OpenAI Codex/GPT-6 on 2026-09-17.

This source records understood, spoken and signed counts, age in days and months, and sex coded 1 for male and 2 for female. The pooled view assigns the 416-item Oxford CDI ceiling and sets `produced` equal to spoken. That field is not a speech-or-sign union. Age in complete months follows the source-preparation rule described in [the data overview](readme.md).
