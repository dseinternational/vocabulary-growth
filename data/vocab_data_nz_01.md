# Vocabulary data - New Zealand

> [!NOTE]
> Revised with assistance from OpenAI Codex/GPT-6 on 2026-09-17.

The Foster-Cohen New Zealand source records repeated assessments using the 675-item NZCDI. It supplies no comprehension count. Its `spoken`, `signed` and `spoken_signed` fields are mutually exclusive cells: speech only, sign only and both. The pooled view adds the both cell to each modality to obtain total spoken and signed counts. Their union is the sum of all three cells. The joint signing engine uses the composition within produced words; it cannot construct an understood-only cell from this source.
