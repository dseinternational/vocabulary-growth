# Vocabulary data - Italy

> [!NOTE]
> Revised with assistance from OpenAI Codex/GPT-6 on 2026-09-17.

The Italian source records understood and spoken counts, with a form identifier and separate form maxima. The pooled view uses `form_max_spoken` as the administration ceiling and does not supply a signed count. Missing comprehension remains missing. See the Italian study description in [the report](../docs/report/methods-data.qmd) and the mapping in `vocab_growth.data_utils`.
